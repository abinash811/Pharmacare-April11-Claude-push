# Product Registry

The single-glance list of every product ever forked from Engineering OS. If you (or anyone else) want to know "what has this company actually built," this file is the answer — no digging through repos required.

Kept in the canonical Engineering OS repo. When a new product forks the OS ([`workflow/00-new-product-intake.md`](workflow/00-new-product-intake.md)), add a row here at Idea stage and keep it current through the product's life. A product fork also keeps its own copy of its own row for local reference, but this canonical copy is the source of truth for the portfolio view.

## How to read this table

- **Status**: `idea` → `building` → `live` → `paused` → `sunset`
- **OS Version**: the Engineering OS version this product was forked from (see [`VERSION.md`](VERSION.md)) — tells you how stale its copy of the OS is and whether [`governance/os-sync.md`](governance/os-sync.md) is due
- **Monthly Cost**: actuals once live, estimate while building — ties to [`governance/cost-governance.md`](governance/cost-governance.md)
